# Delivery and Missing Package Policy

## Package Marked Delivered but Not Received

When a customer reports that a package is marked delivered but
has not been received, support must verify the order and delivery
records.

The investigation should check the delivery timestamp, recorded
delivery location, carrier notes, and available proof of delivery.

A delivered status alone does not establish that the customer
personally received the package.

If the package cannot be located, the case must be investigated
before a refund or replacement is approved.

## Delayed Delivery

When an order remains in transit beyond its estimated delivery
date, support should verify the latest carrier status and estimated
delivery information.

If tracking information is missing or inconsistent, the case
must be referred for further investigation.

An order in transit must not be described as delivered.
