# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the reason for the complaint,
the order status, the applicable return conditions, and the
outcome of any required investigation.

A refund must not be promised solely because a customer
requests one.

## Missing Package Refunds

For packages marked delivered but not received, support must
first complete the missing-package investigation.

A refund may be considered only after the investigation confirms
eligibility and the required approval is obtained.

## Refund Processing

The refund processing time depends on the applicable payment
method and the company's verified refund procedure.

If no confirmed processing timeline is available, support must
not invent a date or promise when the money will arrive.
