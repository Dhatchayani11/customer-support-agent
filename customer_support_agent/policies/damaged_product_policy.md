# Damaged and Incorrect Product Policy

## Damaged Products

When a customer reports a damaged or defective product,
support should collect the order details, the reported damage,
and any required supporting evidence.

The applicable policy must be checked before confirming
replacement or refund eligibility.

## Wrong or Missing Items

When a customer receives an incorrect product or reports
missing items, support should compare the order contents
with the reported issue.

The investigation must distinguish between an item missing
from the shipment and a shipment that has not arrived.

## Resolution Approval

A replacement or refund must not be represented as approved
until eligibility has been verified and the required approval
has been obtained.
